About this project

FASHION is a three-page student website created for the MTM6201 midterm. It explores how bold typography, bright yellow accents and expressive photographs can bring a clothing website to life.

The homepage is based on “Shopping Website” by Nickelfox Design. Collections and About are original extensions of that landing page. Images were extracted from the supplied PDF mockup.

This website demonstrates HTML and CSS. It does not operate a store, issue vouchers, collect email addresses or provide a mobile app.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I start with an outfit?

Choose one piece you enjoy wearing. Build around its colour, shape or texture, and keep the other pieces simple.

How can I make a basic outfit more interesting?

Try a colourful coat, a contrasting accessory or a different combination of pieces you already own.

Can I buy products or download the app here?

No. The products, offers and app illustration belong to the design reference. This is a student HTML and CSS project.
